# Community Code Of Conduct

The LiveFaceSwap AI public community should be useful, respectful, and safe for people discussing creative camera workflows and synthetic media.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific when reporting a problem.
- Protect the privacy and consent of people shown in images or video.
- Redact personal, account, payment, and security-sensitive information.
- Accept constructive correction and keep discussions focused on product improvement.
- Clearly distinguish verified behavior from assumptions or requests.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
- Sharing personal media without authorization.
- Guidance or content intended for impersonation, fraud, non-consensual sexual content, deceptive identity use, or evading safeguards.
- Publishing credentials, private customer data, vulnerability details, or another person's identifying information.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Private concerns can be sent to [support@livefaceswap.ai](mailto:support@livefaceswap.ai).
