# LiveFaceSwap AI Security Policy

Do not report vulnerabilities, exposed credentials, account-access problems, or sensitive synthetic-media abuse details in public issues.

Send a private report to [support@livefaceswap.ai](mailto:support@livefaceswap.ai) with:

- the affected URL or product surface;
- concise reproduction steps;
- the observed and potential impact;
- relevant environment details;
- redacted logs or screenshots; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not include access tokens, passwords, payment details, unredacted personal media, or customer data. Please avoid destructive testing, unauthorized account access, service disruption, or downloading data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue.

We will acknowledge actionable reports and coordinate next steps privately. Public disclosure should wait until the issue is understood and a reasonable remediation window has been agreed.
